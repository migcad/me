The most powerful message your "survival" mind will be sending you is to "shift optimisation from long-term to short-term":
- you are alone
- you are the only one who feels like this,
- it's never going to get any better

But remember:
- no mental state is a permanent state
- there is so much help out there, it starts by putting up your hand. It starts with telling someone "I'm not doing that great"

The best way to decrease anxiety is to seek seeing the world through a constructive proxy mind rather than ours which is in a downwards spiral:
- only message: everything is going to be OK, everything will resolve by itself <-- end of spectrum, not constructive
- constructive proxy mind + establishing a plan of action (hope & grinding: goal + continuous feedback + continuous pivoting) <-- sweet spot
- only message: the world is going to end <-- opposite end of the spectrum, not constructive
