# What would need to happen in our society for AI becoming a decentralised marketplace with economic activity proportionally significant to the economic activity of current AI centralised marketplaces like Hugging Face?

This is a very deep question because it asks whether **AI can follow a
similar decentralisation trajectory to money (Bitcoin), computation
(cloud → edge), and software collaboration (GitHub)**.

The short answer:

> For a decentralised AI marketplace to become economically comparable
> to centralised ecosystems like Hugging Face, society would need to
> shift from treating AI models as "software products" into treating AI
> production as a **global economic coordination problem involving
> compute, data, models, agents, and human contribution**.

Today, Hugging Face succeeds because it solves a coordination problem:

> "How do millions of researchers and developers share, improve,
> evaluate, and deploy AI models?"

A decentralised AI marketplace would need to solve a much larger
problem:

> "How do billions of participants contribute intelligence-production
> resources and receive economic value without a central intermediary?"

The biggest barrier:

Today:

``` text

NVIDIA

Cloud providers
```

A decentralised system requires:

``` text

home computers
```

Similar to:

# Strategic conclusion

If Hugging Face represents:

> **"GitHub for AI models"**

then the decentralised AI opportunity is:

> **"The global economic operating system for intelligence
> production."**

The winning ecosystem will likely combine:

-   Hugging Face (model layer)
-   Bittensor (intelligence marketplace)
-   AIOZ Network (decentralised AI infrastructure)
-   Akash / Render (compute marketplace)
-   Filecoin (data layer)
-   Blockchain incentives (value distribution)

The largest upside exists if AI evolves from:

> "companies building models"

into:

> "society collectively producing intelligence as an economic resource."

That transition would create a market potentially larger than today's
cloud computing market.
